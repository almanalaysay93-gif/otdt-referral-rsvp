Why this matters

Every missed referral is a transplant that never happened.

Fragmented referral pathways between hospitals, ICUs, and transplant coordinators cost Mindanao viable donors and recipients every year. This conference brings together the people who touch every link of that chain — to align on a shared, standardized referral protocol and strengthen the regional donation and transplantation network from the ground up.

Organized by SHARE Organ Transplant Services Unit, Southern Philippines Medical Center.
